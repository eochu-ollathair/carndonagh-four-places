Four recorded places near Donagh Cross

Donagh Cross is the famous stop, but the old church area has four connected records. This small list places them together. Download the four recorded positions for a spreadsheet or map.

Donagh Cross

The carved cross stands with pillar stones. Read its place page and source.

Donagh Church

The present church reused older carved stone. Read its place page and source.

Marigold Stone

This carved cross-slab has crosses, figures, knots and a seven-rayed marigold. Read its place page and source.

St Patrick's Bed

This is a recorded location, not a confirmed visitor stop; its stones had been removed before the survey. Read its place page and source.

Positions are not a route. A dot does not show an entrance or give permission to cross land. Access to St Patrick's Bed is unconfirmed. Check current access before visiting.
